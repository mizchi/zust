# Region (lifetime) checking, part 34: `impl Trait + 'static` arguments, `'static` bounds of fields' types, what a `Cell` stores, a value's drop that sees a loan of itself (issue #1)

Date: 2026-11-25

Part 33 ([2026-11-25-05](2026-11-25-05-region-check-33.md)) was merged with #160. This step adds:

- the lifetime bounds of an `impl Trait` argument (`impl Debug + 'static`), which a caller must meet (E0310);
- E0310 for a field that passes a type parameter to a type of the program that bounds it by `'static`;
- the deref coercion of an array's elements to the element type the context gives;
- what the prelude's `Cell`, `RefCell` and `OnceCell` store through `&self` (`set`, `replace`), and a `Vec`'s
  element as a place;
- E0597 for a value whose drop may see what it borrows, holding a loan of itself;
- the minor finding of #160's last review.

## 1. What was added

- **The lifetime bounds of an `impl Trait` argument.**
  - `fn bar(d: impl Debug + 'static)` called as `bar(d)` from `fn foo(d: impl Debug)` is rustc's E0310: the
    argument's type must outlive `'static`, which nothing says of foo's `impl Debug`.
  - The signature's summary lifts an `impl Trait` argument to a hidden type parameter (`__impl0`). Its trait bounds
    were kept, but its lifetime bounds did not reach the outlives the region check reads, so the call was accepted.
    They do now, and E0310 is at the argument, rustc's position.
  - The message names the parameter as rustc does: "the parameter type `impl Debug` may not live long enough".
- **`'static` bounds of the types a field names.**
  - `struct Foo<U> { bar: Bar<U> }` with `struct Bar<T: 'static>` is rustc's E0310 at the field. rustc infers
    `U: 'a` from a field's `&'a U` (RFC 2093), but not `U: 'static`: the struct must declare it.
  - zustc read the bounds of the other struct nowhere. The definitions of the program's structs and enums are known
    once all items are summarized, and a field's type that names one which bounds a parameter by `'static` is
    checked against what the item declares: inline (`U: 'static`), in a where clause, or through a trait that may
    imply it (`U: Any`, which is not followed).
  - Also in an enum's variants, through other types (`Vec<Bar<U>>`), and in a struct written in a fn body
    (rfcs/rfc-2093-infer-outlives/dont-infer-static.rs).
  - A fn's parameter of such a type stays accepted: rustc takes a signature's types as well formed.
- **The deref coercion of an array's elements.**
  - `[&x, x]` with `x: &[u8]` where `[&[u8]; 2]` is expected, `[&x; 1]`, and `[&s, "b"]` of a `String` as
    `[&str; 2]` are coercions rustc makes at each element. zustc rejected them as mismatched types.
  - The elements of an array literal and the element of a repeat are coerced by deref now, where the context gives
    the element type.
  - This is the type check half of #160's last finding. Its borrow check half is carried here too: the element of a
    repeat is followed as the reference it copies, as an array literal's elements are.
- **What a cell stores.**
  - `d1.0.set(Some(&d2))` stores the borrow of d2 in d1. When d1's drop may see it (a `Drop` impl) and d2 is dropped
    first, that is rustc's E0597 at `&d2`.
  - zustc followed a store through `&mut self` (`v.push(&x)`), and through a `&self` method of the program whose
    self type is invariant in a lifetime an argument is borrowed for. Not through the prelude's cells, whose
    `set(&self, v: T)` names no lifetime.
  - `set` and `replace` of the prelude's `Cell`, `RefCell` and `OnceCell` now store what their argument borrows in
    the cell.
  - An index into the prelude's `Vec` gives its element's type, so `c.v[0].cell` is a place in c, and a store through
    it goes into c (span/dropck_vec_cycle_checked.rs, span/vec-must-not-hide-type-from-dropck.rs,
    rfcs/rfc-1238-nonparametric-dropck/reject-ex1.rs).
  - span/dropck_direct_cycle_with_drop.rs and span/dropck_arr_cycle_checked.rs pass with the stores alone.
- **A value's drop that sees a loan of itself.**
  - `d.0.set(Some(&d))` with `D: Drop` is rustc's E0597: d's drop may see the borrow while d is being dropped.
  - zustc took the loan for dead with its holder. When a value whose drop may see what it borrows is dropped, a loan
    of itself that it holds is now E0597 at the borrow.
  - A type with no `Drop` is accepted, as in rustc: its drop sees nothing.
  - span/issue-25199.rs passes, where `container.store(test)` stores a borrow of `container` in itself.

## 5. Not covered yet

- **The other errors of a cycle of cells.** In `d1.p.set(Some(&d2)); d2.p.set(Some(&d1));`, rustc also reports `d1`:
  the cells' invariance makes d1's and d2's lifetimes one. zustc reports the first, `d2`.
- **E0713 for a borrow through a `Vec`'s element** (rfcs/rfc-1238-nonparametric-dropck/reject-ex1.rs): rustc says "borrow
  may still be in use when destructor runs"; zustc reports E0597 on the same line.
- **Methods of `impl dyn Trait`** (regions/regions-static-bound-on-type-param.rs,
  suggestions/impl-on-dyn-trait-with-implicit-static-bound.rs): such an impl is for `dyn Trait + 'static`, which
  rustc requires of the receiver. zustc's type check does not find these methods yet.
- **A field's bound through a projection** (`<T as Dummy<'a>>::Out` with `impl<'a, T: 'a> Dummy<'a> for T`,
  rfcs/rfc-2093-infer-outlives/regions-enum-not-wf.rs).
- **Outlives through a higher-ranked bound** (`for<'a> G: Outlives<'a>`, nll/local-outlives-static-via-hrtb.rs).
- From part 33 and before: see [part 33](2026-11-25-05-region-check-33.md), section 5.
