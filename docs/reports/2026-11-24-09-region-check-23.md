# Region (lifetime) checking, part 23: placeholders against regions from outside, a placeholder's bound's type arguments, diverging initializers, type aliases with a parameter twice (issue #1)

Date: 2026-11-24

Part 22 ([2026-11-24-08](2026-11-24-08-region-check-22.md)) was merged with #124. This step adds:

- a placeholder of a closure's signature that a region from outside it exempts only when that region is known to
  outlive it;
- a method of a placeholder's bound whose trait's type arguments name lifetimes, in a generic fn that nothing calls;
- the well-formedness of a `let`'s annotation whose initializer diverges;
- a type alias annotation with a hole whose parameter appears twice in the alias.

## 1. What was added

- **A placeholder is exempt only where a region from outside is known to outlive it.** `take(x, |_, _| x)` with
  `F: for<'b> FnOnce(&'b u32, &'a u32) -> &'b u32` and `x: &'x u32` was accepted ("lifetime may not live long
  enough" in rustc). A closure's signature with a region from outside it (the callee's `'a`) exempted every
  placeholder from having to be outlived by the function's regions: what the bound implies might relate them, as in
  `impl FnOnce(&mut &mut &'a i32)` of closures/explicit-closure-lifetime-issue-87290.rs. The exemption is now only
  for a placeholder that a region from outside is known to outlive, by the signature's implied bounds: `&'b u32`
  beside `&'a u32` implies nothing of `'b`, so `'x` would have to outlive every `'b`. This came from #111's review.
- **A method of a placeholder's bound whose trait's type arguments name lifetimes.** Two programs were accepted:
  - `f.call(&x)` with `F: FnLike<&'a isize, &'a isize>` and a local x (E0597 in rustc): the argument is a
    `&'a isize`, so x must outlive `'a`;
  - `let y = f.call(&x); x = 5; drop(y);` with `F: for<'a> FnLike<&'a isize, &'a isize>` (E0506,
    higher-ranked/trait-bounds/hrtb-identity-fn-borrows.rs): the result is of the argument's region.

  Three changes made this work:
  - A generic fn that nothing calls is checked with placeholders, but not one that calls a method of a bound's
    trait whose declaration names no lifetime. Now it is checked where the bound's arguments name lifetimes.
  - The placeholder's method's trait parameters that its arguments do not give are the bound's arguments now: `R`
    of `fn call(&self, arg: A) -> R` failed its typeck ("cannot infer type parameter").
  - The region check takes the trait's type arguments' trees from the bound as written. A lifetime the function
    does not name is the bound's own (`for<'a>`, which the summaries do not keep): one fresh region for the call,
    which the arguments share.
- **A `let`'s annotation is well formed when its initializer diverges.** `let _: &'a S<'b> = loop {};` was accepted
  ("lifetime may not live long enough" in rustc: `'b` must outlive `'a`). A diverging initializer left the `let`
  before its region check. Now the annotation's tree and its well-formedness are taken as for a `let` with no
  initializer, both for a named binding and for `_` (regions/regions-outlives-projection-container-wc.rs, -hrtb.rs,
  regions-assoc-type-in-supertrait-outlives-container.rs).
- **A type alias annotation with a hole, its parameter twice in the alias.** `let x: DoubleCell<_> = make_cell(&b);`
  with `type DoubleCell<A> = Cell<(A, A)>` was accepted (E0597 in rustc, nll/relate_tys/var-appears-twice.rs). The
  hole's regions were fresh at each place. Now the alias's body is instantiated with one tree per parameter, the
  argument's (a hole's fresh), which the parameter's occurrences share. Under `Cell` they are invariant, so `&'b u32`
  must be the `&'static u32` beside it. `DoublePair<_>` (a tuple, covariant) is accepted, as in rustc. The alias's
  own regions are fresh, as the summaries keep no lifetimes of its definition.

## 4. Review

CodeRabbit's review had 4 findings, each checked with zustc and rustc 1.97. All 4 held. Two were false errors this
part brought in (rustc and main accept the programs). Checking them showed one more of the same kind. All are fixed in
e1a16ea6:

- **Two bounds of one trait** (`F: Take<&'a u32> + Take<&'b u64>`). `f.take(x)` with `x: &'b u64` took the first
  bound, so `'b: 'a` was required: a false error. A trait's bound that is one of two or more is not taken now, in
  typeck and in the region check.
- **A bound whose argument binds its own lifetimes** (`F: Get<for<'b> fn(&'b u32) -> &'b u32>`). The new placeholder
  instance gave a false E0308. `'b` was also taken as the bound's own lifetime, one region for the call. Now only the
  lifetimes outside such a `for<..>` count as the bound's own. No placeholder instance is made where a bound names
  none: a local that holds the call's result would take the fn pointer's bound regions as its own.
- **A literal meeting a placeholder's method's types** (found while checking the above). `let a = 1; *f.get() == a`
  with `F: Get<&'z u32>` and `fn get(&self) -> A` was a false E0308: the literal was `i32`. The `let`'s inference now
  types a placeholder's method's arguments and result as its bound's trait declares them, with the trait's
  parameters taken from the bound's arguments, as it does for `dyn`. This also removes a false E0308 that main has:
  `let x = 3; f.get(&x)` with `F: for<'a> Get<'a>`.
- **A diverging `let` whose annotated type needs `Drop`** (`let x: Box<&'a S<'b>> = loop {};`). It was accepted
  ("lifetime may not live long enough" in rustc): the binding of such a type took another branch. Its annotation is
  checked too now.
- **The alias test's positive cases** were in the same `check` as the error, which could hide a false error after
  it. They are a check of their own now.

tests/regionck.rs checks the cases (`part23_review`, `part23_alias_param_twice`). With the fixes and main (a6b265c, #122) merged in, tests/ui shows no regression, and no passing run-pass test is
rejected that main accepts. The KPI workflow measures the pushed head against main.

The second review had 5 findings, 3 of them outside the diff. Each was checked with zustc and rustc 1.97:

- **`for` in a type's name** held, and is fixed in the commit that follows e2e78530. With
  `F: Get<Bufferfor<'a>>`, the scan for a `for<..>` binder took `for<'a>` in `Bufferfor<'a>` for one. The bound
  then named no lifetime of its own, and no placeholder instance was made: the body's double move (E0382 in rustc)
  was not reported, as on main. `for` counts as the keyword now only where no identifier goes on before it, in both
  scans (driver2's and the region check's, one function now).
- **The placeholder exemption with an unrelated external region** did not hold. In the review's program
  (`take(|_p| x)` with `F: for<'b> FnOnce(&'b &'a u32) -> &'b u32` and `x: &'c u32`), rustc 1.97 accepts it: the call
  can take `'c` for `'a`, and then `'c: 'a: 'b`.
- **The run-pass ratchet (1858) above the report's count (1857)** was about an earlier text of the report: main and
  this work pass 1858, as section 2 says since e2e78530.
- **An item's `#[allow(warnings)]` over the FFI lint** held (main reports a false error, rustc accepts it). It is in
  main's code from #122, not in this part's diff, and not about regions. It is left to that work.
- **The explicit `Debug::fmt` fallback in typeck** is in main's code too. A program with impls for `S<u8>` and
  `S<u16>` that calls `fmt::Debug::fmt` on `&S<u16>` prints what rustc's does, so it was not reproduced.

tests/regionck.rs checks the first (`part23_review`).

## 5. Not covered yet

- **A called generic fn with such a bound** (`call_repeatedly(Id)` with `F: FnLike<&'a isize, &'a isize>`): the
  instances it is called with are checked, with the impl's method, which says nothing of the bound's regions. Only
  a generic fn that nothing calls is checked with placeholders.
- **A false E0308 on main and here**: `let x = 3; f.get(&x)` with `F: for<'a> Get<'a>` and
  `trait Get<'a> { fn get(&self, arg: &'a isize) -> &'a isize; }` (rustc accepts it). The placeholder's method's
  argument of an unsuffixed literal local is `&i32` where the method takes `&isize`.
- **A fn pointer annotation whose result is a hole** (`let f: fn(&u32, _) -> _ = first::<u8>;`, E0308 "one type is
  more general than the other" in rustc): the hole cannot name the region the pointer binds.
- **A type alias annotation outside a region check that is on**: `let x: DoubleCell<_> = make_cell(&b);` in a fn
  that names no lifetime and writes no `'static` is not checked.

- **A projection's normalized type at a call** (`test(&x, &&3)` with `fn test<'a>(x: &'a u8, _wf: <&'a u8 as Tr>::Ty)`,
  implied-bounds/assoc-ty-wf-used-to-get-assoc-ty.rs line 24): the callee's summary keeps no codes of a parameter
  whose type is a projection, so the call does not follow it. Inside the fn, and in a `let`'s annotation, it is
  followed now.
- **A cast to a type alias of a fn pointer type** (`c as F` with `type F = fn(&u32);`): the summaries keep no
  lifetimes of an alias's definition (section 4).
- **A closure's `ref` parameter borrowed for a region its result escapes with** (`.filter_map(|ref line|
  self.make_entry(line))`, borrowck/cannot-return-ref-to-fn-param-in-filter-map.rs line 6).
- **A fn item passed through a type alias** of a fn pointer type that fixes a lifetime: `want_G(baz)` with
  `type G = for<'cx> fn(&'cx S) -> &'static S;` (regions/regions-fn-subtyping-return-static-fail.rs). The summaries
  keep no lifetimes of an alias's definition, and a fn's summary does not see its module's aliases.
- **A fn pointer type's distinct bound lifetimes** (`for<'a, 'b> fn(&'a u32, &'b u32) -> &'a u32`) are one
  placeholder where a fn item is passed: the region codes keep no binder (part 19, section 4).
- **A closure made in the function and called in another closure's body**
  (adt-tuple-struct-calls.rs's `annot_reference_named_lifetime_across_closure`). The inner body is checked on its
  own, without the outer closure's signature.
- **What the calls of a closure through a struct's field, or in another closure's body, borrow**
  (closures/issue-113087.rs). Only a closure called by a local's name holds it.
- **A constant's initializer that nothing uses** (`const z: &'static isize = { let p = 3; &p };` in main,
  static/issue-18118.rs), and a fn item in a block of a constant's initializer (borrowck/borrowck-fn-in-const-c.rs).
  A constant is expanded and checked where it is used.
- **A trait's constant whose type names the trait's lifetimes** (`<T as Foo<'a>>::C`, `T::C` with `T: Foo<'a>`,
  nll/user-annotations/constant-in-expr-trait-item-2.rs, -3.rs). A generic fn that nothing calls does not reach the
  constant's use.
- **An implicit reborrow of a `&mut` read through a guard temporary** (`let val: &_ = x.borrow().0;` used after,
  borrowck/issue-36082.rs).
- **`Cow<'_, T>` of a type that is not a slice or `str`**, and `use std::option::Option::Some as S;` in a fn
  (lifetimes/temporary-lifetime-extension-tuple-ctor.rs).
- **A `&mut` moved by value elsewhere than a `let`'s initializer**: `f(a[0])` with a generic f, `vec![a[0]]` (E0508).
- From part 17:
  - two derefs of the program's written in one place (`(&mut **w).bump()`);
  - a trait whose impls for a type are ambiguous (borrowck/mut-borrow-in-loop-2.rs).
- From part 16:
  - a generic fn's `dyn Tr<T>`;
  - `&*vec![1].iter().next().unwrap()`;
  - the opaque type's member constraints (E0700);
  - `&mut` in a tuple's inference;
  - per-field regions;
  - the borrow check of a `static` closure.
