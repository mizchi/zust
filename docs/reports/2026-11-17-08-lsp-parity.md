Date: 2026-11-17

The speed comparison (lsp_compare above) does not look at what is returned. This queries the 3 servers on the same target at the same
positions and compares with rust-analyzer's answers: the set of documentSymbol names, the match of the top 20 workspace/symbol results,
the definition target, hover and the references count at 40 identifiers in the file, and whether completion right after `.` (`x.|name`)
and in the middle of an identifier (`na|me`) includes the name written there.

Findings:
- (At this point) zustc lsp has only diagnostics and documentSymbol / workspace/symbol. It lacks definition, hover, references and completion
  (which rust-analyzer and rust-glancer have), so the speed comparison is not a comparison of the same features.
- Symbols are mostly the same. The documentSymbol differences are the handling of extern blocks (zustc lists the declarations inside at the
  outer level; rust-analyzer makes `extern "C"` one symbol) and how kinds are assigned. The workspace/symbol differences are in ordering
  (how candidates matching the query are chosen and ordered); the top 20 match is 61–87%.
- rust-glancer has definition, hover and completion, but on the zustc target many of its answers differ from rust-analyzer (references
  count match 42%). completion takes about 250 ms per call.
