# Region (lifetime) checking, part 27: two-phase argument reborrows, `ptr::swap` of raw pointers, a placeholder's associated type through its bound's method (issue #1)

Date: 2026-11-24

Part 26 ([2026-11-24-12](2026-11-24-12-region-check-26.md)) was merged with #145. This step adds:

- a call's implicit `&mut` argument reborrow as a two-phase borrow, also without the region check;
- `ptr::swap` of two raw pointers, apart from `mem::swap`'s two `&mut`;
- the check with placeholders of a generic fn that calls its bound's method returning an associated type of its
  `Self`, where its signature names a lifetime or `'static`;
- the program's names as written in two messages, and a correction of part 26's report.

## 1. What was added

- **Two-phase argument reborrows.** Part 26 made `take(x)` with `fn take(_: &mut i32)` borrow `*x` at the call
  without the region check. The borrow ended at the argument, so `f(x, &*x)` with `fn f(_: &mut i32, _: &i32)` was
  accepted; rustc reports E0502.
  - In rustc, the `&mut` reborrow of a call's argument is a two-phase borrow. It is reserved while the other
    arguments are evaluated, and taken when the call is made.
  - Without the region check, that reborrow is now reserved: a shared loan, held until the call. It is taken, as a
    unique borrow, once the arguments are evaluated. A method's `&mut self` was already done this way.
  - `f(x, *x)` and `push(v, v.len())` read `*x` while it is reserved, and are accepted, as in rustc. `f(x, &*x)`
    conflicts when the borrow is taken (E0502), and `f(x, g(x))` while it is reserved (E0499).
  - A `&` parameter's shared reborrow is held until the call too: `f(x, x)` with `fn f(_: &i32, _: &mut i32)` is
    E0502.
- **`ptr::swap` of raw pointers.** `ptr::swap` and `std::ptr::swap` resolved to the prelude's `mem::swap`,
  `__core_swap(&mut T, &mut T)`. This had three effects:
  - with the two-phase reborrows, `ptr::swap(test, test)` became two unique borrows of `*test`. This work's first
    cycle rejected ptr_ops/ptr-swap-overlapping-regions.rs, a run-pass test;
  - `std::ptr::swap(pa, pb)` with raw pointers gave "cannot find value";
  - a call outside `unsafe` was accepted, where rustc reports E0133.
  - It is the prelude's `__core_ptr_swap(*mut T, *mut T)` now, an unsafe fn as in std. `mem::swap(x, x)` is still
    E0499.
- **A placeholder's associated type through its bound's method.** Take `fn bad1<T: Iter>(v: T) -> Box<dyn X +
  'static>` with `fn into_item(self) -> Self::Item` in `Iter`. Returning `Box::new(v.into_item())` was accepted;
  rustc reports E0310 (regions/regions-close-associated-type-into-object.rs).
  - A generic fn that nothing calls is checked with placeholders. A fn whose body calls a method its bounds' traits
    declare is left out: its typeck is the cost, and its check says little of the regions.
  - A method that returns an associated type of its `Self` (`Self::Item`) does say something. The placeholder's
    associated type has a universal region of its own (part 25). A fn that calls such a method, and whose signature
    names a lifetime or `'static`, is checked with placeholders now.
  - It is not done for every fn that calls such a method. That cost +4.9% of the traits KPI, as the generic fns of
    bench/gen_traits.py call such methods. Where the signature names no lifetime, a requirement that only a callee
    makes is not found (section 5).
- **Names in messages.**
  - E0133 of a `ptr::swap` call names `ptr::swap`.
  - The region check's messages name a type or trait that shares its name with one of the prelude's as the program
    writes it: `<T as Iter>::Item`, not `<T as __u_Iter>::Item`.
- **Part 26's report, section 1.** It said that assigning a self-reborrow to its local kills the new value's loans
  of what the local points to. Since part 26's second review the new value keeps what the old value borrowed, as
  section 4 there says. CodeRabbit pointed this out in its last review of #145.

## 5. Not covered yet

- **A requirement on a placeholder's associated type that only a callee makes**, in a fn whose signature names no
  lifetime (`need(v.into_item())` with `fn need<U: 'static>(_: U)`): such a fn is not checked with placeholders.
- **A where clause on a type that holds a placeholder's associated type** (`where Box<T::Item>: X`, the `bad2` and
  `bad4` of regions/regions-close-associated-type-into-object.rs): typeck of the placeholder instance does not read
  it. Closing a `Box<T::Item>` into a `dyn X` then fails to typeck, and the fn is not checked.
- From part 26 and before: see [part 26](2026-11-24-12-region-check-26.md), section 5. `f(x, &*x)` is covered now.
