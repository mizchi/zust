# Region (lifetime) checking, part 31: errors in closures at the closure, a const's block that borrows its own local, moves out of a temporary scrutinee, the use rustc reports of a moved value, a block's value read before its drops (issue #1)

Date: 2026-11-25

Part 30 ([2026-11-25-02](2026-11-25-02-region-check-30.md)) was merged with #156. This step adds:

- an error in a closure's body that has no position of its own, at the closure;
- E0597 for a const's or a static's initializer block that ends in a borrow of its own local;
- a move through a `&` pattern out of a scrutinee that is no place (`match (l1, l2) { (_, &[hd, ..]) => .. }`);
- of the uses of what one move moved, the one rustc reports;
- what a `collect` into a written `Vec<_>` yields, known at its `let`;
- a block's value that is a `Copy` aggregate read out of what the block drops, read before the drops (a fix of the
  code zustc generates).

## 1. What was added

- **An error in a closure's body with no position of its own.** Take a move out of a reference by a closure parameter's
  pattern: `.filter(|&(&_k, &_v)| true)` over a `HashMap<String, i32>`. zustc reported it at the first line of the fn
  the closure is in, as "in `main::{closure#0}::call`". rustc reports it at the closure.
  - Such an error is at the closure's line now.
  - Four tests pass with it, each with an error rustc reports in the closure:
    - liveness/liveness-issue-2163.rs;
    - moves/do-not-suggest-removing-wrong-ref-pattern-issue-132806.rs;
    - pattern/type-alias-as-variant-pattern.rs;
    - pattern/usefulness/refutable-pattern-in-fn-arg.rs.
- **A const's or a static's block that borrows its own local.** Take `const Z: &'static isize = { let p = 3; &p };`.
  The value is `'static`, and `p` goes away at the end of the block. rustc reports E0597 at `&p`; zustc accepted it.
  - It is E0597 now, also through fields and indexes (`&p.0`, `&p[0]`), and for a const or a static written in a fn
    body, at the line it was written on.
  - A borrow of a literal (`{ let _p = 3; &5 }`) is promoted to a static and is accepted, as in rustc.
  - consts/const-eval/generic-slice.rs, consts/issue-78655.rs and static/issue-18118.rs pass.
- **A move through a `&` pattern out of a temporary scrutinee.**
  - Take `match (l1, l2) { (&[], &[hd, ..]) => .. }` with `l1, l2: &[String]`. The scrutinee is a temporary tuple,
    and its patterns had no place to be checked against. zustc accepted the move of `hd` out of the slice behind the
    reference; rustc reports E0508.
  - Such a scrutinee now has a hidden local of its own. It is made where a pattern binds by value under a `&` and none
    binds by `ref`.
  - The move out through the reference is now an error at the scrutinee, as for a local. It is on rustc's line
    (E0507 / E0508).
  - A `Copy` binding (`(_, &Some(n))` with `n: u32`) and a `ref` binding are accepted, as in rustc.
  - Three tests pass:
    - match/slice-move-out-error-12567.rs;
    - pattern/by-move-pattern-binding.rs (`match &s.x { &E::Bar(identifier) => .. }`);
    - traits/trait-object-destructure.rs. Its line is rustc's, but its message is a move error, not rustc's E0033
      (a trait object cannot be dereferenced).
- **The use rustc reports of a moved value.**
  - Take `foo` moved in an inner loop that a `continue` leaves. `foo` is used again in that loop's next iteration
    (`foo == *bar`) and after the loop (`qux.push(foo)`).
  - rustc reports one use per move: the first in its MIR's reverse postorder, where the way out of a loop comes before
    the body. That is the use after the loop. zustc reported the first by line.
  - Where several uses of what one move moved are errors, the one kept now is in the fewest of the natural loops the
    move is in, then the first in a reverse postorder of the blocks. zustc's loops over a range test at their end, so
    the loops decide first.
  - moves/nested-loop-moved-value-wrong-continue.rs passes. A loop over a range, a `while`, a loop over a slice and
    the nested loops are on rustc's lines.
- **What a `collect` into `Vec<_>` yields.** Take `let parts: Vec<_> = s.split('|').collect();`, then `&parts[0]`
  returned as a `&'a str`.
  - zustc's inference took the element type from that use: `&parts[0]` as `&str` made the elements `str`. Only at the
    end of the fn did it unify them with the iterator's `&str`, and that failed.
  - So the `&&str` was taken for a `&str` borrowed from `parts`: a false E0515.
  - rustc selects the `FromIterator` impl at the `let`. The elements are the iterator's items there now.
  - A `&&T` unified with a `&T` (a deref coercion) also no longer makes the two references one type.
  - The make_entry of borrowck/cannot-return-ref-to-fn-param-in-filter-map.rs is now accepted, as in rustc.
- **A block's value read before its drops.** This is a fix of the code zustc generates, found while checking the cases
  above against rustc.
  - Take `fn f() -> (i64, i64) { let v = vec![(1, 2)]; v[0] }`. The value of the block was the address of the
    element, read after `v` was dropped. On main it printed garbage, and `v[0]` of a `Vec<&str>` crashed.
  - The same was true of `*b` and `b.0` of a Box, of `return v[0];`, and of the deref coercion of a `&&str` local
    returned as a `&str`.
  - Such a value (a `Copy` aggregate, not a fresh temporary, where the block drops something) is copied out before the
    drops now, as rustc evaluates the tail first. The deref coercion to the type expected is done before them too.
  - A value at a stack slot's address (a local's, a match result's) is not copied, as no drop frees a stack slot. The
    IR tells which values those are.
  - A new driver2 probe, tests/probe/tail_copy_out.rs, builds and runs these cases. Its expected output is rustc 1.97's.

## 4. Review

- **Main moved.** #127 was merged while the PR was open, and its compile-fail count was 4684. After main (b642ce0) was
  merged into the PR, the count is 4695: the same eleven new tests. There are 0 regressions, no newly rejected run-pass
  test, and `cargo test` passes.
- CodeRabbit reviewed the PR at 6fd0ccd and raised four findings. Each was checked with zustc and rustc 1.97, and all
  four are fixed:
  - **A `collect` in parentheses.** `let parts: Vec<_> = (s.split('|').collect());` still gave the false E0515. The
    check of `collect` now looks through the parentheses.
  - **References of different depths.** A `&&&u8` passed as a `&&u8` was given the type `&&u8`, so `***c` was
    "`u8` cannot be dereferenced". rustc accepts it.
    - Where the two reference chains end in known shapes of different depths, the references are not linked, and
      only the innermost types are unified.
    - Before, only a difference of one level was seen.
  - **A const's borrow through a local reference.** `let p = &DATA; &p.0` borrows `DATA`, not the local, but it was
    E0597.
    - A field or an element of a local now counts as its own storage only where the local holds the aggregate: a
      written type that is no reference, or a value built there.
    - `let p = (3,); &p.0` is still E0597, as in rustc.
  - **A move out of a `&mut`.** The message said "shared reference": the check took `Deref(true)` (a Box's deref) for a
    `&mut`'s, which is `Deref(false)`. It says "a mutable reference" now.
  - tests/regionck.rs checks the four cases (`part31_review`).

## 5. Not covered yet

- **Moves in generic bodies that call a bound's methods.** `x.zero()` twice with `fn zero(self)` of `T: Foo` is no
  error while `T` is only instantiated with `u32`, or not at all. A generic fn is checked with placeholders only where
  its body calls no method of its bounds' traits, as that costs its typeck (+4.9% of the traits KPI). Such a fn is
  checked per instance instead (binop/binop-consume-args.rs, moves/issue-34721.rs).
- **A false E0373 for a closure that calls a method through a captured reference.** Take
  `v.iter().filter_map(|_| h.mk()).collect()` returned from a fn with `h: &'r H<'r>`. zustc reports that the closure
  borrows `h`; rustc accepts it.
  - The capture of `h` is of `h`, not of `*h`.
  - The collected value is taken to hold what the closure holds.
- **A closure's result that borrows its own parameter through a call.** `filter_map(|ref line| self.make_entry(line))`
  is rustc's E0515 (borrowck/cannot-return-ref-to-fn-param-in-filter-map.rs). zustc accepts it.
- **Spans in macros.** An error in a `macro_rules!` expansion is at the call; rustc points into the macro's
  definition (borrowck/span-semicolon-issue-139049.rs).
- **`@` bindings.** The moves and borrows of `a @ NC(b, c)` and `ref a @ b` are not checked
  (pattern/bindings-after-at/*).
- From part 30 and before: see [part 30](2026-11-25-02-region-check-30.md), section 5.
