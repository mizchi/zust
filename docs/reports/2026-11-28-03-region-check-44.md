# Region (lifetime) checking, part 44: placeholders' universes and outlives, and the binders of fn pointer types in trait obligations (higher-ranked bounds, 2)

Date: 2026-11-28

Part 43 ([2026-11-28-02](2026-11-28-02-region-check-43.md)) was merged with #350: a call's bound that selects one
of the program's impls relates the impl's head to the goal, and a lifetime the bound binds (`for<'a>`) is a
placeholder of the call. A placeholder was a universal region with no relation to the others, so the check found
an impl not general enough only where the placeholder had to be another universal region or `'static`. This part
takes the rules a placeholder keeps that the universal regions do not, and the binders of fn pointer types.

## 1. What was added

- **Universes.** A placeholder is of a universe the regions made before its obligation cannot name: no region of
  the goal's type the caller had (an inference variable too) may be it. `want_hrtb::<&u32>()` with `T: for<'a>
  Foo<&'a isize>` and `impl<'a> Foo<&'a isize> for &'a u32` makes the reference's region the placeholder: "not
  general enough", as in rustc. Part 43 found it only where that region was the caller's named lifetime.
- **A placeholder outlives only itself.** It contains no point of the body (rustc's `check_bound_universal_region`):
  a placeholder that must outlive any region it is not equal to is "not general enough".
  `impl<'a, 'b: 'a> Foo<'b> for FooS<'a>` for `F: for<'b> Foo<'b>`, `test::<FooS>(..)`
  (higher-ranked/trait-bounds/due-to-where-clause.rs): the placeholder `'b` must outlive FooS's region. That a
  region must outlive a placeholder is left alone: of the outer universe, it may be 'static.
- **The binders of fn pointer types.** A region a fn pointer type of the goal binds is none of the impl's but one
  the impl's head binds at that place: `impl<'a> X for fn(&'a ())` and `impl<A> Foo for fn(A)` are not general enough
  for `fn(&())` / `fn(&i32)`, which are `for<'r> fn(&'r ..)`. Nor is a region the goal names (a lifetime of the
  callee, `'static`, a placeholder) one the head binds: `impl X for fn(&())` is no impl for `fn(&'a ())`. Such a
  region is known in two places:
  - a type argument written with a fn pointer type (`indirect::<fn(&())>()`): its regions are those the type binds;
  - a bound's argument (`T: Trait<for<'b> fn(fn(&'b u32))>`): the codes the bound writes.

  A region inference made is either, so it is not taken. A head with a fn pointer type that has a type parameter of
  the impl in it (`impl<A> Foo for fn(A)`) has regions to relate now (TraitDb::lt_traits).
