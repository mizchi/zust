# Region (lifetime) checking, part 7: control flow, temporaries and what prelude methods return (issue #1)

Date: 2026-11-22

Part 6 ([2026-11-22-02](2026-11-22-02-region-check-6.md)) was merged with #43. This step takes the cases its report
left out where the borrow check's graph, temporaries and the prelude's results were not followed.

## 1. What was added

### Borrow check

- **Constant conditions:** the borrow check's graph takes both edges of a constant condition, as rustc's does:
  `false || { i = 5; true }` may leave i uninitialized (E0381). Before, a constant condition took one edge.
- **Chains of `&&` / `||`:** `a && b && c` (one operator) is lowered as one chain, each operand's short-circuit to the
  end, so c runs only after b did: `true && { z = 3; true } && z == 3` reads z after it is set, as rustc's control flow
  says.
- **`&*Box::new(1)`:** a borrow of the deref of a temporary a call makes is a loan of that temporary, as `&f()` is:
  stored where it outlives the statement (`m.insert(42, &*Box::new(1))`) it is E0716. A `let` extends it.
- **Receiver temporaries:** in `let x = mk().get();` with `fn get(&self) -> &A`, the receiver is a temporary freed at
  the end of the statement: a use of x after it is E0716. Not when the method's result does not borrow self
  (`mk().len()`, a result `&'static str`).
- **What a prelude method returns:** a method of the prelude's types whose result holds references holds what its
  receiver holds (the prelude writes no lifetimes). Returning `it.next()` of a local's iterator, or
  `raw.iter().map(..).collect()`, is E0515. `copied` / `cloned` take a reference off: the result holds what is under it,
  not the borrow (`parts.first().copied()` holds what parts holds).
  - A returned chain of method calls is reported where it starts, as rustc's span of the returned value is.
- **Move closures and captures not `mut`:** a capture held by value of a local not declared `mut`, which the body
  assigns (or borrows mutably, or calls a `&mut self` method of), is E0594 / E0596 at the mutation. Not a mutation
  through a reference the capture holds (`t.0.0 = x` with `t.0: &mut S`).

- **Calls of a type parameter's closure:** a placeholder bounded by `Fn(..)` / `FnMut(..)` / `FnOnce(..)` is callable
  in its instance, against the bound: its arguments checked against the bound's parameters, its result a value of the
  bound's result type. `FnMut` borrows the callee mutably (E0596 when it is not `mut`), `FnOnce` takes it (E0382 when it
  is called twice), `Fn` reads it. Such bounds no longer keep a fn from its placeholder instance.

### Region check and signatures

- **Type aliases written without their lifetimes:** `b: A` with `type A<'a> = &'a isize` is one marker for what the
  alias stands for; its regions are elided (fresh ones in a parameter), as `&isize`'s. Before, such a parameter had no
  tree and was not followed.
- **E0195 for impls of types that are not structs:** the methods of `impl<'a> Foo<'a> for &'a isize` are checked
  against the trait's declarations as a struct's are: the method's own early-bound lifetimes and their bounds.
