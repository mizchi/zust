# Region (lifetime) checking, part 19: closures' calls and requirements, fn items as higher-ranked fn pointers, impl constants' lifetimes (issue #1)

Date: 2026-11-24

Part 18 ([2026-11-23-07](2026-11-23-07-region-check-18.md)) was merged with #105. This step adds:

- the results of a closure's calls, which hold what the arguments its body makes them of borrow;
- what a closure's body requires of its parameters, required of its calls' arguments;
- closure bodies that write a lifetime, checked with the function's regions;
- a fn item passed where a fn pointer type binds its regions;
- an impl's constants whose types name the impl's lifetimes.

## 1. What was added

### Closures

- **A closure's call holds what the arguments its body makes its result of borrow.** These were accepted:
  - `let some = |a| Some(a); let g = some(&temp());` with g used after (E0716);
  - `let some = Some; let g = some(&temp());`, the constructor written as a value (E0716,
    lifetimes/temporary-lifetime-extension-tuple-ctor.rs's case);
  - `let g = some(&x); drop(x);` with g used after (E0505).

  rustc infers one signature for such a closure, its result's regions tied to its parameters' by its body. Here the
  parameters the body's value is made of, as written, are taken where the closure is made: the parameter itself, a
  constructor's argument, a `&` of a place in it, a view of it (`s.as_str()`), each arm of an `if` or a `match`. A
  closure that only reads or calls with a parameter (`|k| m.get(k)`, `|a| a.len()`) holds nothing of it.
- **What a closure's body requires of its parameters is required of its calls' arguments.** `f(&c)` with c a local
  was accepted for each of these (E0597):
  - `let f = |x| { let y: &'static u32 = x; };`;
  - `let f = |x| W::<&'static u32>(x);`, `let f = |x| id::<&'static u32>(x);`;
  - `let f = W::<&'static u32>;`, the constructor of `struct W<T>(T)` as a value.

  The annotation or the turbofish names regions, and the closure's parameter flows into them. Its shared signature
  (the regions its calls' arguments flow into) has to outlive them, so the argument's loan does.
- **A closure's body that writes a lifetime is checked though nothing else asks for it.** A closure was region
  checked only for a bound, an annotated parameter or a capture. `|| { let c = 66; let y: &'static u32 = &c; }` was
  accepted (E0597), and so was `let y: &'a u32 = &c;` with the function's 'a. A body that writes a lifetime in a
  `let` annotation, a cast or a turbofish is checked now, nested closures included: `let f = S::<&'a u32>; f(&c);`
  in a closure's body is E0597. nll/user-annotations/adt-tuple-struct-calls.rs passes with these.

### Region check

- **A fn item passed where a fn pointer type binds its regions must do for each of them.** `want(baz)` with
  `fn want(f: for<'cx> fn(&'cx S) -> &'static S)` and `fn baz(x: &S) -> &S` was accepted (E0308 in rustc; "lifetime
  may not live long enough" here). The regions the parameter's type binds were fresh variables, and the item's value
  took its result's elided regions as fresh ones too. They are one placeholder of the call now, and the item's value
  elides as its signature does (`for<'a> fn(&'a S) -> &'a S`). `fn foo(x: &S) -> &'static S` is fine for
  `for<'cx> fn(&'cx S) -> &'cx S`. A `let` annotation (`let g: for<'cx> fn(&'cx S) -> &'static S = baz;`) is checked
  the same way.
- **An impl's constant whose type names the impl's lifetimes is of the regions its path writes.** `<Foo<'a>>::C`
  returned as `&'static u32`, with `impl<'a> Foo<'a> { const C: &'a u32 = &22; }`, was accepted ("lifetime may not
  live long enough", nll/user-annotations/constant-in-expr-inherent-1.rs). The summary keeps the codes of such a
  constant's type against the impl's lifetimes. A use gives them the regions the path writes on the self type
  (`Foo::<'a>::C` too), and a fresh one where it writes none.

## 4. Review

CodeRabbit's review of #109 had 5 findings, one of them outside the diff. Each was checked with zustc and rustc 1.97
on a program that shows it. Four held and are fixed and tested (`part19_review`). Two of them were errors this work
had brought in:

- **A call through a fn pointer local took its regions once for all calls.** `(g(a), g(b))` with
  `let g: fn(&u32) -> &u32 = id;`, returned as `(&'a u32, &'b u32)`, was a false "lifetime may not live long
  enough". The item's elided result now tied to its parameter, so both calls related `'a` and `'b`. The regions a fn
  pointer's or item's type binds are taken afresh at each call now: its own lifetimes, those elided in it, those
  `for<..>` binds. An annotation's elided result is its one parameter region, as elision makes it. With a named
  lifetime (`fn id<'x>(x: &'x u32) -> &'x u32`), main had the same false error.
- **A `let` that shadows a closure's parameter.** `let x = &C; let _y: &'static u32 = x;` in `|x| ..` still
  required `'static` of the parameter: `f(&local)` was a false E0597. A `let` ends what the body requires of the
  name it binds.

Two were on main too:

- `S.take::<&'static u32>(&c)` in a closure's body was accepted (E0597): a method's turbofish did not make the body
  checked. It does now, as a path's does.
- `Foo::<&'b u32>::C` with `impl<'a> Foo<&'a u32> { const C: &'a u32 }` returned as `&'static` was accepted. The
  finding said a valid use (`&'b u32`) was rejected, which did not hold; the missed error did. The path's self type
  is now matched against the impl's whole, type arguments included.

One holds and is left for later (section 5): a fn pointer type's distinct bound lifetimes are one placeholder.
`want(choose)` with `fn want(_: for<'a, 'b> fn(&'a u32, &'b u32) -> &'a u32)` and
`fn choose<'s>(_: &'s u32, y: &'s u32) -> &'s u32` is accepted (E0308 in rustc; main accepts it too). One
placeholder for all of them adds no error that distinct ones would not; telling them apart needs the binder in the
region codes.

The second review had 1 finding, checked the same way. It held, was on main too, and is fixed and tested
(`part19_review`): what a method's turbofish in a closure's body writes was not required of the closure's calls.
`f(&local)` with `let f = |x| S.take::<&'static u32>(x);` was accepted (E0597). The method is looked up where the
receiver's type is known as the closure is made (a local's, or a struct's path), and the parameter the argument is
passed to gives the turbofish's type.

The `test` job failed once on the first fixes: tests/parse.rs's `parses_like_syn` counts the crate's sources with
zust's parser and with syn, and zust's parser reads a destructuring assignment as a `let`. regionck.rs writes two
plain assignments there now.

## 5. Not covered yet

- **A fn item passed through a type alias** of a fn pointer type that fixes a lifetime: `want_G(baz)` with
  `type G = for<'cx> fn(&'cx S) -> &'static S;` (regions/regions-fn-subtyping-return-static-fail.rs). The summaries
  keep no lifetimes of an alias's definition, and a fn's summary does not see its module's aliases.
- **A fn pointer type's distinct bound lifetimes** (`for<'a, 'b> fn(&'a u32, &'b u32) -> &'a u32`) are one
  placeholder where a fn item is passed: the region codes keep no binder (section 4).
- **A closure made in the function and called in another closure's body**
  (adt-tuple-struct-calls.rs's `annot_reference_named_lifetime_across_closure`). The inner body is checked on its
  own, without the outer closure's signature.
- **A trait's constant whose type names the trait's lifetimes** (`<T as Foo<'a>>::C`, `T::C` with `T: Foo<'a>`,
  nll/user-annotations/constant-in-expr-trait-item-2.rs, -3.rs). A generic fn that nothing calls does not reach the
  constant's use.
- **A closure whose result is tied to a parameter the bound binds** but returns what it captures
  (`take(|_z| x)` with `F: FnMut(&isize) -> &isize` and `x: &'x isize`, lifetimes/closure-lifetime-bounds-10291.rs).
  A region of the function is not required to outlive the bound's region there. This is in progress for the next
  part.
- **An implicit reborrow of a `&mut` read through a guard temporary** (`let val: &_ = x.borrow().0;` used after,
  borrowck/issue-36082.rs).
- **`Cow<'_, T>` of a type that is not a slice or `str`**, and `use std::option::Option::Some as S;` in a fn
  (lifetimes/temporary-lifetime-extension-tuple-ctor.rs).
- **A `&mut` moved by value elsewhere than a `let`'s initializer**: `f(a[0])` with a generic f, `vec![a[0]]` (E0508).
- From part 17:
  - two derefs of the program's written in one place (`(&mut **w).bump()`);
  - a trait whose impls for a type are ambiguous (borrowck/mut-borrow-in-loop-2.rs).
- From part 16:
  - a generic fn's `dyn Tr<T>`;
  - `&*vec![1].iter().next().unwrap()`;
  - the opaque type's member constraints (E0700);
  - `&mut` in a tuple's inference;
  - per-field regions;
  - the borrow check of a `static` closure.
