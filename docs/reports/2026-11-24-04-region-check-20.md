# Region (lifetime) checking, part 20: closures against the regions their bounds bind, boxed closures' signatures, captured locals' regions, what a closure's calls borrow (issue #1)

Date: 2026-11-24

Part 19 ([2026-11-24-01](2026-11-24-01-region-check-19.md)) was merged with #109. This step is about closures:

- a closure that returns what it captures where its bound's result is tied to a region the bound binds;
- a closure boxed where a `Box<dyn Fn(..)>` is expected, the trait object its signature;
- the regions of a captured local, from outside the closure;
- a parameter annotated with one of the function's lifetimes where the bound binds the region, or where a fn
  pointer type does;
- what the calls of a closure whose parameter has one region for all of them borrow, held by the closure.

## 1. What was added

- **A closure returning what it captures where its bound's result is tied to a bound region.** `take(|_z| x)` with
  `F: FnMut(&isize) -> &isize` and `x: &'x isize` was accepted ("lifetime may not live long enough" in rustc). The
  result's region is the parameter's, which the bound binds, so `'x` would have to outlive every region. A region of
  the function that had to outlive one the bound binds was never reported, as the callee's lifetimes in the bound
  might relate them. That holds only when the signature has regions from outside it (the callee's lifetimes, as in
  `impl FnOnce(&mut &mut &'a i32)` of closures/explicit-closure-lifetime-issue-87290.rs); with none, it is an error.
- **A closure boxed where a `Box<dyn Fn(..)>` is expected takes the trait object as its signature.**
  `drop::<Box<dyn for<'z> FnMut(&'z isize) -> &'z isize>>(Box::new(|z| x))` was accepted, and so was the same in a
  `let` annotated with that type: `Box::new` gave its argument no expected type when the box's content is a trait
  object, so the closure had no bound to be checked with. A closure argument takes the trait object now, as
  `&|x| ..` where a `&dyn Fn(..)` is expected does (lifetimes/closure-lifetime-bounds-10291.rs).
- **A captured local's regions are from outside the closure.** `ay = z` in a closure whose parameter z is a `&isize`
  its bound or its annotation binds, with `ay = &y` a captured local, was accepted (E0521 "borrowed data escapes
  outside of closure" in rustc). The regions of a capture that are not universal in the function were fresh
  variables in the closure's body; they are regions from outside it now, which a region its signature binds must
  not flow into (regions/regions-nested-fns.rs).
- **A parameter annotated with one of the function's lifetimes where the bound binds the region.**
  `closure_expecting_bound(|x: &'x u32| ..)` with `F: FnOnce(&u32)` was accepted ("lifetime may not live long
  enough" in rustc): the bound's codes were taken and the annotation's lifetimes left aside. Where the bound's region
  is one it binds and the annotation names one of the function's lifetimes or `'static`, that lifetime would have
  to be every region there: it is related both ways to a placeholder of its own. Such an annotation starts the
  function's region check if it was off (`take(|_x: &'static u32| {})` in a fn that names no lifetime)
  (closures/closure-expected-type/expect-region-supply-region-2.rs).
- **The same where a fn pointer type binds the region.** `let _: fn(&()) = |_: &'a ()| {};` was accepted ("lifetime
  may not live long enough" in rustc, nll/user-annotations/issue-54124.rs), and so was
  `let g: fn(&u32) = |_: &'static u32| {};`. The annotation's fn pointer type gives the codes of the closure's
  parameters: a region it binds (elided in it, or `for<'x>`) is a placeholder, related to the annotation's lifetime
  as for a bound. A closure passed to a parameter of such a type is checked the same way
  (`want(|_: &'static u32| {})` with `fn want(f: fn(&u32))`), the parameter's codes from the callee's summary, or all
  bound where the callee names no lifetime. An annotated parameter that names a lifetime starts the function's region
  check here too.
- **A closure whose parameter has one region for all its calls holds what they borrow.** `g(&a); a = 2; g(&a);`
  with `let g = |x| { let _: &u32 = x; };` was accepted (E0506 in rustc), and so was `f(&a)` in a loop with
  `let f = |_| {};` made before it (E0597). A closure made where nothing was expected, a parameter of it a
  reference, has one region there for all its calls (part 19), and that region is in the closure's type: it lasts
  while the closure is used. A call through a local's name now flows what the arguments to such parameters borrow
  into that local. A closure in a struct's field is left aside: the field's bound may give it its signature
  (`S { f: |x| .. }` with `F: Fn(&u32)`).

## 4. Review

CodeRabbit's review of #111 had 1 finding, checked with zustc and rustc 1.97 on a program that shows it. It held and
is fixed and tested (`part20_closure_fnptr_annotated`): a reference to a struct with no lifetime parameters was
skipped where a fn pointer type binds the region. `let _: fn(&S) = |_: &'static S| {};` was accepted ("lifetime
may not live long enough" in rustc), and so was `want(|_: &'static S| {}, &1)` with
`fn want<'b>(_f: fn(&S), _y: &'b u32)`. The path `S` is written with a mark that stands for as many regions as the
struct has lifetime parameters, none here, so the codes did not match the annotation's. The marks are left out now
where the codes then match the type's regions, as for a parameter's `'static`.

The second review had 2 findings, checked the same way. Both held, were in what this part added, and are fixed and
tested (`part20_closure_fnptr_annotated`, `part20_closure_holds_call_args`):

- `(|_: &'static u32| {}) as fn(&u32)` was accepted: a cast's type is checked against the closure's annotated
  parameters now, as a `let`'s annotation and a parameter's type are. A closure stored in a local and then cast
  (`let c = |_: &'static u32| {}; c as fn(&u32)`, E0308 in rustc) is not checked yet: the cast does not see the
  closure's parameters.
- `(g)(&a); a = 2; (g)(&a);` was accepted (E0506): the callee is stripped of parentheses before it is matched as a
  local's name.

The third review had 1 finding, which held and is fixed and tested (`part20_closure_holds_call_args`):
`let r = &g; r(&a); a = 2; g(&a);` was accepted (E0506 in rustc). What the call's arguments borrow flowed into r, the
reference, not into g, the closure whose type holds the region. A new event of the borrow check, `HoldThrough`, makes
the locals a local's references point to hold the loans, shared references too (`Store` follows only `&mut` ones).
The call emits it where the callee is a local of a reference type.

## 5. Not covered yet

- **A fn item passed through a type alias** of a fn pointer type that fixes a lifetime: `want_G(baz)` with
  `type G = for<'cx> fn(&'cx S) -> &'static S;` (regions/regions-fn-subtyping-return-static-fail.rs). The summaries
  keep no lifetimes of an alias's definition, and a fn's summary does not see its module's aliases.
- **A fn pointer type's distinct bound lifetimes** (`for<'a, 'b> fn(&'a u32, &'b u32) -> &'a u32`) are one
  placeholder where a fn item is passed: the region codes keep no binder (part 19, section 4).
- **A closure made in the function and called in another closure's body**
  (adt-tuple-struct-calls.rs's `annot_reference_named_lifetime_across_closure`). The inner body is checked on its
  own, without the outer closure's signature.
- **What the calls of a closure through a struct's field, or in another closure's body, borrow**
  (closures/issue-113087.rs). Only a closure called by a local's name holds it.
- **A constant's initializer that nothing uses** (`const z: &'static isize = { let p = 3; &p };` in main,
  static/issue-18118.rs), and a fn item in a block of a constant's initializer (borrowck/borrowck-fn-in-const-c.rs).
  A constant is expanded and checked where it is used.
- **A trait's constant whose type names the trait's lifetimes** (`<T as Foo<'a>>::C`, `T::C` with `T: Foo<'a>`,
  nll/user-annotations/constant-in-expr-trait-item-2.rs, -3.rs). A generic fn that nothing calls does not reach the
  constant's use.
- **An implicit reborrow of a `&mut` read through a guard temporary** (`let val: &_ = x.borrow().0;` used after,
  borrowck/issue-36082.rs).
- **`Cow<'_, T>` of a type that is not a slice or `str`**, and `use std::option::Option::Some as S;` in a fn
  (lifetimes/temporary-lifetime-extension-tuple-ctor.rs).
- **A `&mut` moved by value elsewhere than a `let`'s initializer**: `f(a[0])` with a generic f, `vec![a[0]]` (E0508).
- From part 17:
  - two derefs of the program's written in one place (`(&mut **w).bump()`);
  - a trait whose impls for a type are ambiguous (borrowck/mut-borrow-in-loop-2.rs).
- From part 16:
  - a generic fn's `dyn Tr<T>`;
  - `&*vec![1].iter().next().unwrap()`;
  - the opaque type's member constraints (E0700);
  - `&mut` in a tuple's inference;
  - per-field regions;
  - the borrow check of a `static` closure.
